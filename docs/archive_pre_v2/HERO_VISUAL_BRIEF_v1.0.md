# 0. PURPOSE
# 0. 문서 목적

이 문서는 리본소프트 홈페이지의 Hero 영역에 사용할 **대표 비주얼 한 장의 방향, 역할, 금지 요소, 구도, 심리적 목표**를 명확하게 정의한다.

This document defines the **direction, role, prohibitions, composition, and persuasive objective** of the single hero visual used on the Rebornsoft website.

이 Hero 비주얼은 단순 장식 이미지가 아니다.

It is not decorative artwork.

이 비주얼은 방문자가 **3초 안에 리본소프트가 어떤 회사인지 직관적으로 납득하도록 만드는 핵심 설득 장치**다.

It is the key persuasive device that should help a first-time visitor understand **within three seconds** what kind of company Rebornsoft is.

# 1. Hero가 수행해야 할 일

The hero must communicate the following three ideas as quickly as possible.

### KR

### KR

### EN
Rebornsoft connects **technology with real environments and real experiences**.

## 03
### KR
리본소프트는 산만한 잡다한 회사가 아니라, 정제된 기술적 신뢰를 가진 회사다.

Rebornsoft should feel technically credible, intentional, and professionally composed.

# 7. NON-NEGOTIABLE SCENE ELEMENTS
# 7. 반드시 포함되어야 할 장면 요소

Hero는 아래 네 요소를 동시에 전달해야 한다.

The hero must combine the following four elements at once.

---

## 7.1 PHYSICAL SPACE
## 7.1 실제 공간

### KR
추상 배경이 아니라 실제 공간처럼 느껴지는 건축적 환경이 있어야 한다.

### EN
The scene must feel like a real architectural environment, not an abstract background.

### Required cues
- spatial depth
- floor / wall / volume
- believable materials
- designed lighting
- realistic perspective

---

## 7.2 DIGITAL LAYER
## 7.2 디지털 레이어

### KR
공간 안에 디지털 시스템이 스며들어 있어야 한다.

### EN
Digital systems must be present as part of the environment.

### Required cues
- integrated display surfaces
- responsive media
- subtle interface-like layers
- sensing / control hints
- not a floating sci-fi hologram spectacle

---

## 7.3 HUMAN PRESENCE
## 7.3 인간의 존재

### KR
사람이 아주 작게라도 존재해야 한다.

### EN
At least one or two human figures should be present, even subtly.

### Why
사람이 있어야 “기술”이 아니라 “경험”으로 읽힌다.

Human presence makes the image read as experience rather than pure technology.

---

## 7.4 SYSTEMIC ORDER
## 7.4 시스템적 질서

### KR
기술 요소들이 산만하게 흩어진 것이 아니라 하나의 작동 구조처럼 느껴져야 한다.

### EN
The technologies should feel coordinated within one working system, not scattered features.

### Required feeling
- connected
- integrated
- intentional
- engineered
- calm

---

# 8. 장면의 정서

# 10. COMPOSITION STRATEGY
# 10. 구도 전략

## Layout principle
텍스트와 이미지가 싸우지 않아야 한다.

Copy and image must not compete.

## Recommended composition
### Desktop
- image covers full hero background or full hero stage
- primary subject weighted to the right or center-right
- left side kept more open / darker for typography
- deep space extends into right/background
- clear text-safe zone on left 42–48%

### Mobile
- dedicated crop or mobile version
- text sits in lower third
- core subject remains visible above or behind copy
- no critical subject directly beneath headline area

---

# 11. PREFERRED CAMERA / VIEWPOINT
# 11. 권장 시점

## KR
정면 대칭보다 약간 비대칭적이고, 공간의 깊이와 방향성이 느껴지는 시점이 좋다.

## EN
A slightly asymmetrical viewpoint with strong depth and directional space is preferred over a flat frontal composition.

### Preferred characteristics
- medium-wide interior perspective
- one main zone + receding depth
- enough visual calm for large text
- avoids clutter

---

# 12. HUMAN FIGURE GUIDELINES
# 12. 인물 가이드

## Recommended
- 1–2 people maximum
- small or medium-small scale within space
- professional / contemporary silhouette
- engaged with the environment, not posing for camera
- no exaggerated gesturing

## Avoid
- smiling stock-photo business people
- group of many people
- family attraction mood
- religious gathering
- VR-headset gimmick unless very subtle and spatially appropriate

---

# 13. TECHNOLOGY CUES
# 13. 기술 표현 요소

Hero는 “기술 회사” 느낌을 내야 하지만, 기술이 전경의 주인공이 되어서는 안 된다.

The hero should feel technological, but technology should not dominate the image as isolated gadgets.

## Preferred technology cues
- integrated media wall or display surface
- subtle responsive lighting
- environmental interface traces
- projected information plane integrated into the space
- controlled sensing or interaction hint
- soft signal blue accents in limited areas
- media / system glow contained within architecture

## Avoid
- giant floating UI
- hologram storm
- data tunnel
- floating code
- cyberpunk signage
- excessive hardware close-ups

---

# 14. MATERIAL / LIGHTING LANGUAGE
# 14. 재질 및 조명 언어

## Preferred materials
- dark metal
- glass
- matte stone / concrete
- high-quality composite finishes
- refined architectural surfaces

## Lighting
- low-key but readable
- edge lighting
- integrated linear light
- subtle media illumination
- controlled highlights
- not pitch-black
- not over-saturated

## Accent
Signal Blue may appear subtly in:
- responsive edge
- small lighting node
- controlled media line
- interface cue

Accent must not dominate the scene.

---

# 15. COLOR RULE
# 15. 색상 규칙

## Base
- near-black
- graphite
- warm-neutral highlights
- limited cool technology accents

## Do not use
- rainbow gradients
- purple-neon cloud
- bright cyan tech storm
- oversaturated LED carnival
- gold luxury palette

---

# 16. WHAT THE IMAGE MUST NOT BECOME
# 16. 절대 되면 안 되는 것

## It must NOT become:
- a generic AI startup image
- a metaverse homepage hero
- a gaming scene
- a cyberpunk city
- a theme-park promotion visual
- a church / religious environment
- a futuristic control room cliché
- a collage of multiple projects
- a busy infographic-like composition
- a fake “completed Rebornsoft facility”

---

# 17. STRICT PROHIBITIONS
# 17. 엄격 금지 요소

### KR
다음 요소는 절대 넣지 않는다.

### EN
The following elements are strictly prohibited.

- Noah's Ark imagery
- crosses, churches, biblical motifs
- AI brain / neural network iconography
- circuit-board backgrounds
- giant holographic UI in midair
- multiple unrelated scenes in one collage
- company logos inside the image
- text labels inside the image
- stock-business-person vibe
- floating product mockups
- purple/blue mesh gradient backgrounds
- obvious sci-fi cityscape
- excessively cute or futuristic robots

---

# 18. TEXT SAFE ZONE
# 18. 텍스트 안전영역

## Desktop
Left-side text-safe zone must remain calm and relatively uncluttered.

### Recommended safe zone
- left 42–48% width
- enough darkness / reduced detail
- no bright light directly behind headline
- no faces behind headline
- no strongest focal point under supporting copy

## Mobile
- lower-third text area
- enough tonal contrast behind body copy
- avoid busiest visual region behind text

---

# 19. HERO + COPY RELATIONSHIP
# 19. Hero 비주얼과 카피의 관계

이미지는 카피를 반복 설명하지 않는다.

The visual should not literally repeat the wording.

### Correct relationship
Copy says:
**Technology for Interactive Environments**

Image proves:
**what such an environment feels like**

### Wrong relationship
Copy says technology, and the image shows random abstract “technology.”

---

# 20. OUTPUT DELIVERABLES
# 20. 산출물 규격

The hero visual should be produced in at least the following forms.

## Desktop Master
- minimum 3840 × 2160
- ratio 16:9

## Wide-safe source
- crop-safe around 3200 × 1800 or above

## Mobile Master
- minimum 1440 × 1920
- ratio 3:4 or 4:5 recommended

## Additional note
If possible, the desktop and mobile images should be related versions of the same concept rather than unrelated scenes.

# 21. HERO VARIATION STRATEGY
# 21. Hero 후보 전략

최종 한 장을 고르기 전에 2~3개의 성격 차이를 가진 후보안이 필요하다.

Before final selection, 2–3 concept variations should be created.

## Variation A — Recommended baseline
**Balanced corporate-spatial**
- 가장 균형적인 corporate + spatial + technical 방향
- 가장 우선 검토

## Variation B
**More technical**
- sensing / media integration의 힌트가 조금 더 강함
- 단, AI 스타트업처럼 가면 실패

## Variation C
**More experiential**
- 인간 경험과 환경 분위기가 조금 더 강함
- 단, 전시/테마파크 광고처럼 가면 실패

# 23. SELECTION LOGIC
# 23. 최종 선택 원칙

최종 선택 시 가장 화려한 이미지를 고르지 않는다.

Do not choose the most spectacular image by default.

Choose the image that best satisfies:

1. positioning clarity
2. text compatibility
3. spatial credibility
4. controlled technical mood
5. Rebornsoft fit
6. Spectrascape-level discipline

# 24. 승인 조건
