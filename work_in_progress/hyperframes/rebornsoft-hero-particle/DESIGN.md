# Rebornsoft Hero Particle — Visual Direction

## Style Prompt

Preserve the supplied architectural photograph as an immutable, pixel-stable base layer. The only animated material is the existing center-right display content: a restrained but clearly perceptible field of fine blue-white particles that feels like atmospheric airflow, flowing information, and an invisible digital current. The composition should remain near-indistinguishable from the source at any paused frame while the motion reads at normal playback speed, with quiet premium technology character and no added interface language.

## Colors

- `#050A10` — sampled near-black blue architectural shadow; never added outside the source display.
- `#0B1B2B` — deep blue display-field shadow.
- `#315E87` — low-opacity particle body.
- `#78B9EB` — restrained blue-white particle illumination.
- `#D7EEFF` — rare fine particle highlight; never used as a solid fill.

All animated color is clipped to the physical display. No additional brand colors are permitted.

## Typography

No typography is used.

## Composition Rules

- Output: 1920×1080, 10 seconds, 30fps.
- Base source: `HERO_REBORNSOFT_PROTOTYPE_A1_v0.1.png`, byte-identical copy only.
- Camera and base photograph remain completely static for the entire timeline.
- The supplied 1672×941 source is scaled once to the 1920×1080 output canvas without crop, pan, zoom, regeneration, or relighting.
- Animated pixels are constrained to the photographed display quadrilateral and excluded from the standing person silhouette.
- The original display image remains visible beneath the animation.
- Particle placement and motion are seeded and deterministic.
- All cyclic fields return exactly to their initial values after 10 seconds.

## Motion

- Slow, controlled, elegant, quiet, and meditative, but visibly alive at normal playback speed.
- Fine particles traverse the screen cyclically from left to right over one complete loop.
- Layer velocities differ subtly while each layer completes an integer number of seamless spatial cycles.
- Broad deformation uses blended cyclic fields, never a legible sine-wave line.
- Near the standing person’s horizontal position, screen-only particles separate around a soft invisible field and reconnect without markers or pulses.
- No infinite repeats.

## What NOT to Do

- No modification or animation of architecture, lighting, reflections, furniture, or people.
- No generated pixel outside the physical display.
- No camera movement, crop, zoom, pan, parallax, or perspective change.
- No flashing, glitch, cyberpunk neon, hologram, UI, text, icon, chart, ring, pulse, or tracking marker.
- No energetic particle bursts, obvious waveform geometry, or high-contrast pasted-on graphics.
