# Rebornsoft Wide Hero — Visual Direction

## Style Prompt

Use the supplied architectural installation photograph as a locked 1920×1080 base. Animate only an integrated layer of blue-white particles following the existing curved-wall artwork. The field must read clearly as alive at normal playback speed while retaining a quiet, premium, computational atmosphere. Architecture, lighting, reflections, furniture, and the visitor remain photographically stable; the on-screen field bends around the visitor as if sensing presence.

## Colors

- `#050A10` — architectural near-black; base/background only.
- `#10263A` — deep display blue.
- `#3D7BA8` — particle body.
- `#83C8F3` — illuminated particle current.
- `#D9F1FF` — rare particle highlight.

No additional colors and no typography.

## Composition

- 1920×1080, 10 seconds, 30fps, seamless deterministic loop.
- `HERO.png` is copied byte-for-byte and scaled once to the output canvas without crop.
- The curved display uses a multi-point physical boundary mask.
- The table and foreground visitor are subtracted from the animated mask.
- The original display artwork remains fully visible below the generated layer.

## Motion

- Clearly perceptible slow left-to-right information flow.
- Several particle layers use different drift amplitudes and vertical responses.
- The main wave gathers, stretches, separates, and reconnects through cyclic deformation.
- A soft traveling brightness current reveals directional movement without pulses or interface graphics.
- Particles flow around the visitor’s screen-space position without markers.
- Every position, deformation, opacity, and brightness field returns exactly at 10 seconds.

## What NOT to Do

- No camera movement, crop, zoom, parallax, relighting, or architectural motion.
- No particles beyond the curved display.
- No particles over the table or visitor.
- No UI, text, icons, rings, pulses, holograms, glitch, flashing, or cyberpunk effects.
- No random or time-dependent simulation.
