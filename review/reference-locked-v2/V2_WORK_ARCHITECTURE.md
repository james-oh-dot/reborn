# V2 WORK Architecture

## Objective

WORK operates as evidence of capability, not a uniform portfolio gallery. Visual prominence follows evidence strength, physical relevance, and asset quality. Existing copy and factual status remain unchanged.

## Evidence Sequence

1. **Work Statement** — enlarged page opening.
2. **Current Development / Noah** — a dedicated long-form chapter visually separated from completed work.
3. **Major Delivered Experience / Yancheng** — the largest delivered-project scene.
4. **Secondary Delivered Experience** — Hongdae remains supporting due to low source resolution.
5. **Technology Development / Deployment** — Hyundai, FIT-M, and Hana become open editorial rows rather than equal cards.
6. **Systems, Engagements, Proof** — retained as lower-depth documentary history.

## Noah Hierarchy

- Project name and `IN PROGRESS · 2025–2026` lead the chapter.
- Field/Site Inspection is the largest documentary evidence field, but remains explicitly captioned.
- Public Record is smaller secondary context.
- The not-completed disclaimer remains visible.
- The layout does not imply final design, confirmed site, construction, official SCO commissioning, or exact contractual role.
- No empty concept placeholder is visible; the composition can accept an approved concept visual later.

## Yancheng Hierarchy

- Approved 65:35 documentary pairing is retained.
- The media pair occupies the dominant composition.
- `DELIVERED · 2022`, project title, and scope qualifier remain unchanged.
- The presentation supports curved-screen and simulation-content evidence without implying delivery of the full park.

## Secondary Work

- Hongdae is kept below the lead project and not enlarged beyond its source quality.
- Hyundai, FIT-M, and Hana are arranged as open ruled evidence rows.
- Status metadata remains quiet but explicit.
- 7 Cubic and Cityfield remain in their verified lower-level systems/engagement contexts.

## Mobile

Noah and Yancheng media remain large, project titles retain authority, and status/copy follow a deliberate reading order without card accumulation.
