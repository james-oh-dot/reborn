# V2 HOME Architecture

## Objective

HOME operates as one corporate presentation rather than a sequence of preview modules. Existing copy, evidence, page links, status, Hero asset, and KR/EN structure remain unchanged.

## Chapter System

1. **Hero** — preserved as the visual benchmark.
2. **Company Statement / Trajectory** — Evolution becomes a near-viewport corporate statement; DNA is separated as a long trajectory rule.
3. **Core Capabilities** — the former compact sticky sequence becomes three long editorial domains. Each Core receives its own title, statement, evidence field, and scroll duration.
4. **Selected Work / Real-world Evidence** — Yancheng becomes a full-width lead statement. Secondary work is delayed and visually subordinate.
5. **Current Development** — Noah becomes a large current-scale statement before institutional proof, while retaining `IN PROGRESS · 2025–2026` and the same public copy.
6. **Corporate Credibility** — KISA and supporting institutional evidence receive a full chapter rather than a compact proof module. KISA remains historical 2021 evidence.
7. **Closing** — existing corporate contact bridge remains concise.

## Card Reduction

- Core capabilities are no longer three states within one small interface-like frame.
- Yancheng is not a project card; it is a large evidence scene with an overlapping editorial text anchor.
- Technology Proof remains open image/row structure, not badges.
- No new panels, icons, gradients, or decorative assets were introduced.

## Dominant Media Logic

- Hero: existing approved representational visual only.
- CORE 01: Hana UI at contained safe scale.
- CORE 02: 7 Cubic UI at available source resolution.
- CORE 03: Yancheng physical evidence as the strongest domain media.
- Selected Work: Yancheng receives the largest verified physical-evidence field.
- Noah HOME chapter remains statement-led because an approved concept visual is not available.

## Mobile

Each Core remains a long sequential domain, evidence stays substantial, metadata remains secondary, and the page avoids collapsing into equal cards. The desktop negative space is reduced rather than mechanically preserved.
