# Reference Anatomy — Spectrascape Structural Benchmark

Live pages reviewed before implementation:

- `https://spectrascape.tech/`
- `https://spectrascape.tech/markets.html`

Review date: 2026-08-14. This document records relative design grammar only. No source code, copy, proprietary assets, identity, or exact composition is reused.

| Reference Area | Observed Principle | Rebornsoft Equivalent |
| -------------- | ------------------ | --------------------- |
| Hero | One large statement occupies most of the first visual field; support and CTA are clearly subordinate | Preserve the approved Rebornsoft Hero and carry its scale into the first company chapter |
| Capability sequence | Long chapters give each domain enough time to become a distinct world | Treat the three Rebornsoft Core capabilities as three large editorial domains, not compact cards |
| Selected work | A lead project receives substantially more image and scroll space than supporting work | Make Yancheng the dominant delivered physical evidence; subordinate Hongdae, FIT-M, and Hana |
| Markets | Repeated subjects are structured by large headings, rules, and rhythm rather than boxed cards | Use open editorial rows and strong rules for secondary Rebornsoft evidence |
| Section headers | Eyebrow and chapter number orient the user, while a much larger statement carries the message | Keep numbered metadata quiet and make corporate/project statements dominant |
| Media | Images are allowed to operate as large fields and anchors rather than thumbnails | Use existing Rebornsoft evidence at the largest safe size its resolution and truth role allow |
| Closing | The final CTA returns to near-Hero scale and acts as a closing corporate statement | End HOME and WORK with a large, restrained Rebornsoft contact bridge |

## Section Scale

At the observed 1280 × 720 desktop viewport, major HOME chapters occupied roughly 750–1,740px in height. The strongest capability and selected-work chapters were well over one viewport. On 390px mobile these same chapters expanded to roughly 1,400–2,650px rather than collapsing into short cards. Rebornsoft V2 therefore uses long-form chapter fields and deliberate internal staging.

## Typography Hierarchy

The observed hierarchy used a very large Hero statement, major chapter headings at a clearly separate scale, then much smaller support copy and metadata. V2 retains the existing Rebornsoft font system but increases the relative separation between statement, project title, body, and status.

## Negative Space

Space is used to isolate ideas, not merely to inflate padding. Major statements have clear air before the evidence field, and successive subjects are separated by rules or large transitions. V2 gives each Core, Yancheng, Noah, and credibility chapter a distinct visual scene.

## Media Dominance

Strong projects are not presented as thumbnail collections. One image or image pair becomes the primary spatial anchor while supporting details remain secondary. V2 applies this to Yancheng and uses Noah documentary media as bounded evidence rather than a final-project hero.

## Editorial Grid

The reference frequently uses asymmetric widths, narrow text anchors, wide media, and long horizontal rules. V2 uses its own Rebornsoft 12-column editorial grid with open rows and asymmetric evidence fields; it does not reproduce any reference layout.

## Scroll Rhythm

One idea remains active for multiple scroll movements. The user is not rushed through a sequence of previews. V2 restructures HOME as a continuous corporate presentation and WORK as an evidence sequence with clear status categories.

## Evidence Presentation

Confidence comes from hierarchy and specificity rather than badge walls. V2 keeps status as restrained metadata, preserves scope qualifiers, and gives the strongest verified evidence the largest safe visual field.

## Mobile Adaptation

The live reference does not simply shrink desktop. Large headings remain strong, chapters become longer, media stays substantial, and horizontal overflow is avoided. V2 mobile removes simultaneous competition, keeps one dominant idea per sequence, and avoids turning content into stacked card UI.

## Boundary

**COPY THE GRAVITY, NOT THE IDENTITY.** Rebornsoft remains Technology + Experience, not an AV integrator or a Spectrascape imitation.
