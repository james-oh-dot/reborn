# V3 HOME Architecture

V3 HOME uses new semantic HTML rather than the V1/V2 section wrappers.

1. `v3-hero` — approved brand Hero.
2. `definition-intro` + `definition` — Company Definition and DNA trajectory.
3. `capability-chapter` — three numbered `domain-row` articles with alternating media/text composition.
4. `major-work` — Yancheng major case stage.
5. `secondary-evidence` — open ruled evidence ledger for Hongdae, FIT-M, and Hana.
6. `current-development` — statement-led Noah chapter without documentary overclaim on HOME.
7. `credibility` — KISA historical evidence and corporate credibility ledger.
8. `v3-close` — one closing statement and contact direction.

The old standalone Evolution module is absorbed into Company Definition. The DNA remains intact. Cards, badge walls, and equal project modules are not used.

- Yancheng: B — major evidence composition, but source resolution is below ideal.
- Noah: B — authoritative statement; approved concept visual remains missing.
